Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026
In short: Load Shedding has no accounts and we do not collect your name, email or other personal details. Your game progress stays on your device. The app shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google may collect device information to show and measure those ads, as explained below.

Who we are

Load Shedding (the “app”) is a wire-connecting puzzle game for Android published by PureForm Solutions (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information the app handles, why, and the choices you have. If you have a question, contact us at gerald30.munyoro@gmail.com.

Information we do not collect

You do not need an account to play. We do not ask for, collect or store your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location or any other personal information. The app does not use your camera or microphone. We do not run our own servers for the app, so we never receive your game data.

Information stored on your device

To remember your place in the game, the app saves the following on your device only:

  • the levels you have reached, your stars and your coins;
  • your daily challenge streak and the date you last completed it;
  • how many free-coin videos and lucky spins you have used today;
  • whether you have seen the game’s tips, and your settings, such as sound on or off.

This information never leaves your device and we cannot see it. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data in your device settings, deletes it permanently.

Internet check for the lucky spin

The lucky spin only works when your device has a working internet connection. To check this, the app sends a small request to Google’s standard connection-check address (connectivitycheck.gstatic.com), the same kind of check that Android phones make by themselves. The request contains no information about you or your game. Like any internet request, it reveals your IP address to Google, which handles it under the Google Privacy Policy.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and is supported by ads provided by Google AdMob. It shows:

  • a banner ad while you play a level;
  • occasional full-screen ads between levels, and sometimes when you open or return to the app;
  • optional reward videos that you choose to watch, for example for a hint, a power-up, extra time, double coins, free coins, an extra lucky spin or a bonus chest after a boss level.

To serve and measure these ads, and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process information from your device, such as:

  • your device’s advertising ID;
  • your IP address, which may be used to estimate your general location (such as your country);
  • device and app information, such as the device model, operating system, language and app version;
  • how you interact with ads, such as which ads are shown and whether they are tapped;
  • diagnostic information, such as ad loading errors.

Google handles this information under its own policies. You can learn more at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in the Google Privacy Policy.

We ask Google to limit ads in the app to content rated suitable for general audiences.

Your consent and choices

  • Consent where required. If you are in a region where the law requires consent for personalised ads (such as the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland), the app asks for your choice the first time you open it, using Google’s consent tool. Where that applies, you can change your choice at any time with the privacy (padlock) button at the top of the app’s main menu.
  • Personalised ads. On Android you can reset your advertising ID, or delete it to opt out of personalised ads, in Settings > Privacy > Ads (the wording varies by device).
  • Reward videos are optional. You never have to watch one to keep playing.

Age requirement

Load Shedding is intended for people aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information through the app, please contact us and we will help remove it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, laws such as South Africa’s Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA), the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) or US state privacy laws may give you rights over your personal information, such as the right to access, correct or delete it, or to object to its use. Because we do not hold your personal information ourselves, most requests are best handled through the choices above or directly with Google. You are still welcome to contact us and we will help where we can.

Security

Your game data stays on your device. Information collected for ads, and the connection check, are sent to Google over encrypted connections and are protected under Google’s security practices.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app changes. The “Last updated” date at the top shows the latest version. If a change is significant, we will note it in the app’s update notes on Google Play.
